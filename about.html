About Cafe Hill

We prepare unique, affordable African meals for students, professionals and families — inside MUBS in Nakawa.

Since Day One
Our Story

One Table. Many Flavours.

Cafe Hill African Fusion was created to provide affordable, quality meals in a welcoming campus environment in Nakawa. We combine African hospitality, culture and flavour to create memorable dining experiences for individuals, families, students, churches and businesses.

Inspired by years of cooking experience and a passion for innovation, we set out to make good food affordable and accessible — not a luxury reserved for a few.

  • Generous African portions, cooked to order
  • A comfortable place to meet, eat and stay a while
  • Hospitality at the centre of every plate

Where To Find Us

Inside MUBS, in Nakawa.

Cafe Hill sits on the Makerere University Business School campus in Nakawa, Kampala. It is a comfortable stop for a quick lunch, a family meal, a church fellowship or an office team meeting — with takeaway and delivery for anyone further away.

  • On campus at MUBS, Nakawa — easy to reach
  • Seating for individuals, families and groups
  • Takeaway and delivery around Nakawa
