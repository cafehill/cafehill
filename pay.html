How We Confirm a Payment

1. We open our own Mobile Money or payment-provider records and match the transaction ID and amount to your order.

2. We never accept a screenshot alone as proof of payment — the funds must appear in our own records.

3. Once matched, we mark the order as paid and send your receipt on WhatsApp.

↩

Refund Policy

Refunds may be issued when an order cannot be fulfilled as agreed, when we deliver the wrong item, or when we cancel your order after payment.

Approved refunds are returned through the same method you paid with, normally within 3 working days. Because food is perishable, we cannot accept a return of a meal that has already been served — tell us immediately if something is wrong.

Our Reasoning

Why We Chose These Methods

Our customers are everyday Ugandans — students, workers and families — plus offices and churches ordering for groups. That shaped the choice:

  • Mobile money first. MTN and Airtel are how most customers already pay, so ordering stays fast and familiar.
  • Bank transfer for business customers. Offices, schools and churches need a paper trail and an invoice.
  • Cash on delivery builds trust for first-time customers who prefer to pay when the food arrives.
  • Card payment through a licensed provider covers customers who want to pay from outside Uganda, without us ever seeing card details.
  • WhatsApp for the order itself keeps everything in one thread — menu, total, payment and delivery.
₿

What About Cryptocurrency?

We do not accept cryptocurrency. Crypto assets are not legal tender in Uganda, and the Bank of Uganda has repeatedly cautioned the public that cryptocurrencies are not recognised as a means of payment and carry real risk for consumers.

For that reason we stay with the regulated options above: mobile money, bank transfer, cash on delivery and cards through a licensed provider.

i

Your Safety

We will never ask for your Mobile Money PIN, your card CVV, or a login password. If anyone claiming to be Cafe Hill asks for those, it is not us — report it on WhatsApp.
