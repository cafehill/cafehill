Policies & Trust

Plain-language rules for delivery, returns, privacy and terms — plus how we handle your information and the claims we make.

The Rules

How We Treat Our Customers

Short, honest and written in plain language. No small print hidden in a corner.

01 Delivery Policy

Orders are prepared and delivered according to customer requests and agreed schedules.

  • We deliver around Nakawa and nearby areas — your address and fee are confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay.
  • Deliveries only go ahead when someone can receive them at the address given.
  • If we are running late, we tell you before the agreed time.
  • Dine-in and takeaway are available for customers who prefer to collect.

02 Returns & Food Concerns

Food concerns should be reported immediately after delivery.

  • Message us on WhatsApp with your order name and time.
  • Tell us what was wrong: wrong item, missing item, or quality problem.
  • We may ask for a photo so the kitchen can see the problem.
  • Meals that have already been served cannot be physically returned, so we fix the problem with a replacement or a refund.

03 Refund Policy

Refunds may be issued when orders cannot be fulfilled as agreed.

  • Wrong, missing or spoiled items are eligible for a refund or replacement.
  • Approved refunds go back through the same payment method used.
  • Refunds normally complete within 3 working days.
  • See the Order & Pay page for the full refund and confirmation steps.

04 Privacy Notice

Customer information is only used for communication, ordering and service improvement.

  • What we collect: your name, phone number, delivery area and order details — only when you choose to message us.
  • Why: to confirm, prepare and deliver your order, and to answer your questions.
  • Who can see it: only the Cafe Hill order team. We do not sell or share it with advertisers.
  • How long: kept only while the order and any follow-up are open, then deleted.
  • This website itself has no login, no form and no database — it does not store visitor information.
  • Handled in line with Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019. To see, correct or delete what we hold, message +256 751 000 300.

05 Terms & Conditions

  • Prices are in Uganda shillings and are subject to change without notice.
  • Orders depend on availability of ingredients on the day.
  • Customers must give accurate names, phone numbers and addresses.
  • The group lunch package must be booked in advance.
  • This website is for educational purposes — it is a student project for BUC3106 at MUBS.
  • Payments shown on this site are SAMPLE numbers and are not active.

06 Customer Protection

We will never ask for your Mobile Money PIN, your card CVV or a password.

  • We only accept payment confirmation we can see in our own records — never a screenshot alone.
  • If someone pretending to be Cafe Hill asks you for money or PIN details, it is not us: report it on our official WhatsApp and social pages.
  • Orders can be cancelled free of charge before cooking starts.
  • Questions or complaints are answered on WhatsApp, in writing, on the day.
