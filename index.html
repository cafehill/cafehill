Why We Exist

Local Heart. Real Flavour.

Many customers want exciting, high-quality meals but often have to choose between expensive restaurant food and ordinary local food. Cafe Hill bridges that gap.

We combine African food traditions with modern, honest cooking to create affordable, creative and memorable meals for students, professionals and families.

  • Generous African portions, cooked to order
  • Creative meals at student-friendly prices
  • Fresh ingredients and hygienic preparation
  • Dine-in, takeaway, delivery and group catering
